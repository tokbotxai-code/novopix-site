Range 85–100, mean 97.2. A score below 100 means the automated check found at least one issue on the home page; it does not say how serious it is.

How it was measured

  • Sample frame: English local authorities in the ONS Local Authority Districts (December 2024) list: 296 entries, of which 293 could be matched to a council website address through the GOV.UK local authority API (3 could not be matched and were left out).
  • Sampling: simple random draw with a fixed seed. First draw 40 sites; because only 29 gave a full measurement, a second draw of 20 from the remaining sites was added. Total 60 sites attempted.
  • Tool: the NovoPix scanner (Google Lighthouse accessibility category), home page only, at most 2 requests in parallel with pauses.
  • Included in statistics: only the 42 sites with status live_lighthouse. The other 18 returned a partial result (HTML only, no accessibility score) and are excluded, so the sample may differ from all councils.

Limitations

A first automated check finds only part of the accessibility problems. It does not prove compliance or non-compliance with the law or with WCAG. Lighthouse covers a limited set of automated rules, on one page, on one day. The scanner does not report issue types per site, so this study does not give breakdowns by problem type.
