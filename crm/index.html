Mini CRM

Keep track of who you talked to, what they need and when to follow up.

Add your contacts, give each one a status and a next step, and sort by what is due. Everything is saved in this browser only. No account, no server, nothing is sent to us.

Your data stays on this device. Clearing the browser data deletes it, so export a CSV now and then as a backup.

    How it works

    • Everything is stored in your browser (local storage), nothing leaves your device.
    • Export a CSV to back up or open it in a spreadsheet; import it again on another device.
    • Statuses: New, Contacted, Offer sent, Won, Lost. Sort by follow-up date to see what is due first.
    • Do not use it for sensitive categories of personal data. It is a simple contact list, not a records system.
