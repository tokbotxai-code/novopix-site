Our approach

  1. Scope together

    We agree what is in scope, the rules and the timing in writing.

  2. Test and verify

    We test carefully, confirm findings and avoid disruption.

  3. Report and support

    You get prioritised fixes and help to retest.

Principles we work by

Authorised only

Every test starts with written permission and a defined scope.

No jargon

Findings are explained in plain language with clear next steps.

Responsible handling

We keep your data and reports confidential.

Frequently asked questions

Is testing safe for live systems?

We agree timing and limits first and can test a staging copy instead.

Do you guarantee that you will find everything?

No. Testing reduces risk but cannot prove a system is free of flaws.

What do we receive at the end?

A written report with findings, evidence and recommended fixes.
