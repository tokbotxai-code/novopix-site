How we work

Simple rules.

Small teams ship better when the rules are simple.

Tested before it ships

Every change is tried on a phone and on a desktop before it goes out. If it is not ready, it waits.

Private by default

PantryPal keeps your data on your own device. We do not add ads or third-party trackers to our apps.

Plain and honest

Clear wording, real data, no invented numbers. Dates in PantryPal are labels you entered, not safety advice.

07

Questions

Frequently asked questions.

Short answers. If yours is missing, write to us.

What do you build?

Web apps, websites and small custom tools. Our own apps are FridgeBite, PantryPal and the PepSafe reference website.

How do we start?

Send us a short message with your idea using the form below. We reply by e-mail with questions and a plan.

How much does it cost and how long does it take?

It depends on what you need. Starting prices are in the pricing section and on the full pricing page. After we hear your idea we send a plan with a price and a time estimate.

What happens to my data?

Our own apps have no ads and no trackers. For any project we tell you plainly what it collects and why.

Which languages do you work in?

Our apps are in English, Polish and Spanish. Tell us which languages your project needs.

Do you look after a project after launch?

Yes. We can fix problems and keep what we built up to date.
